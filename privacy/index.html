Privacy

This page explains how Exile handles data while providing moderation features in Discord.

What Exile processes

Exile automatically processes Discord message content and related metadata in servers where it operates to detect spam. This can include user, server, channel, and message identifiers; usernames and channel names; timestamps; attachments and stickers; member and permission information; and moderation actions or errors.

This processing is automated and is used to identify spam patterns, not to build a memory or profile of conversations.

How data is used

Exile uses this data to identify and respond to spam, remove affected messages, take configured moderation actions, post a log of what happened, and let authorized moderators review and resolve incidents.

Exile does not sell Discord data, use it for advertising, or use message content to train artificial intelligence models.

Storage

Exile does not maintain a separate archive of ordinary Discord messages. Message data may remain temporarily in runtime memory and service caches while Exile operates.

Hosting systems may retain limited operational logs, such as identifiers, channel names, action results, and error information, for operation and troubleshooting. Exile stores the Discord identifiers and settings needed to operate in each server. Depending on the server's configuration, this may include server, channel, role, and user identifiers; moderation preferences; protection status; and configuration timestamps.

Logs in Discord

When Exile responds to an incident, Exile posts a log inside Discord. The log may contain account identifiers, message evidence, copied images, channel names, and the outcome of moderation actions.

These logs are ordinary Discord messages and remain in the server until someone with appropriate permissions deletes them. Exile does not keep a separate log database.

Sharing

Data is sent to Discord to perform moderation actions and post logs. Service providers used to host and operate Exile may process limited data on the operator's behalf. Exile does not disclose Discord data to advertisers or data brokers.

Information submitted through the contact form is used to respond to your message and may be processed by the site's hosting provider.
